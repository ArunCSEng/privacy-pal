How the Zero-Egress Gateway Works

Most online contact portals utilize third-party SaaS forwarders or server-side relational databases that record visitor IP addresses, browser fingerprints, and cleartext message bodies. BitDark eliminates these attack surfaces by utilizing in-browser client composition:

  • Memory Obfuscation: Operational inbox addresses are assembled dynamically in active memory, preventing automated headless web crawlers from scraping cleartext addresses.
  • Kinetic Motor Verification: To mitigate automated submission abuse without intrusive third-party analytics or cookies, BitDark measures continuous acceleration variance and micro-velocity fluctuations during user interaction.
  • Direct Client Handoff: Verified submissions transfer formatted payloads directly to your active browser session or native operating system email client via RFC 2368 URI standards.
