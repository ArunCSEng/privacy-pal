Zero-Telemetry Privacy Standard

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Governing BitDark.net Operations

🛡️ 1. Fundamental Philosophy & Architecture

At BitDark.net ("BitDark", "we", "our", or "us"), privacy is an architectural guarantee rather than merely a legal statement. BitDark operates as a specialized, zero-telemetry cybersecurity, privacy auditing, and AI data leakage mitigation workspace.

Our core operational mandate is uncompromising: your sensitive credentials, tokens, payloads, and files never leave your client device. We do not run background telemetry collectors, user profiling trackers, or server-side logging databases.

💻 2. 100% Client-Side Execution (Zero Network Egress)

BitDark hosts 100+ cybersecurity tools—including AI Prompt Sanitizers, C2PA Provenance Manifest Readers, EXIF & GPS Metadata Strippers, Offline JWT Debuggers, and Multi-Hash Verifiers.

Unlike conventional online utilities that upload files or payloads to remote cloud servers for parsing, all tools on BitDark.net run entirely in local client volatile memory (RAM) within your web browser:

  • Native W3C Web Cryptography: High-entropy cryptographic hashing (SHA-256, HMAC, AES-256-GCM, RSA) executes locally via crypto.subtle without remote APIs.
  • Local WebAssembly (WASM): Binary inspection, SQLite parsing, and token counting run in isolated sandbox instances on your local processor.
  • Isolated Web Workers: Heavy tasks (such as Argon2 derivation or PCAP packet decoding) execute on background browser threads without dispatching outbound HTTP requests.
  • HTML5 Canvas & Stream APIs: Media metadata removal and file inspection operate exclusively on client-side memory buffers.

🔒 Zero Network Egress Guarantee: You can load any tool on BitDark.net, disconnect your device from the Internet, and continue analyzing data offline. Zero input bytes are ever transmitted across the wire to our infrastructure.

🌐 3. Network Infrastructure & Cloudflare Web Analytics

BitDark.net is routed through Cloudflare as an edge reverse proxy and Content Delivery Network (CDN) to ensure fast global asset delivery, SSL/TLS v1.3 encryption, and DDoS defense.

To measure site stability without compromising your personal privacy, we rely exclusively on Cloudflare Web Analytics:

  • Cookie-less by Design: Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use persistent cookies, client fingerprinting, or device tracking IDs.
  • Aggregate Performance Metrics Only: It collects non-identifiable, aggregate operational metrics such as total page requests, load latency, country of origin, and HTTP status codes (e.g., 404 and 5xx errors) to maintain site health.
  • Zero Behavioral Profiling: We deliberately do not run invasive marketing analytics tools (such as Google Analytics / GA4) to track personal visitor journeys.

Network edge connection logs (such as IP addresses and request headers) may be processed transiently by Cloudflare strictly for network threat mitigation in accordance with the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

📢 4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

To keep all 100+ cybersecurity tools completely free, accessible, and maintained without subscription paywalls, BitDark.net displays digital advertisements served by Google AdSense.

In accordance with Google AdSense Publisher Policies, we disclose the following details regarding advertising technologies on our website:

  • Use of Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to BitDark.net or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the Internet.
  • Ad Privacy & Tool Isolation: Google AdSense scripts run solely to render advertisements in designated page layout slots. Google AdSense does not and cannot access, read, or expose the sensitive data, prompts, keys, or files you process within BitDark's tools.

Visitor Opt-Out Options & Choices:

You have full autonomy over your advertising preferences and cookie settings:

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • You may also configure your web browser to block third-party cookies or utilize browser privacy shields.

💾 5. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

BitDark may store minimal, non-personally identifiable state data in your browser's native localStorage or sessionStorage strictly to remember interface preferences (such as selected category filters, search states, or UI theme modes).

This state data remains strictly inside your own device storage and is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any external backend server. You can wipe this data at any time via your browser's "Clear Site Data" feature.

✉️ 6. Inbound Direct Communications

If you reach out to BitDark Operations via our Contact Gateway: Contact Form, any details you voluntarily disclose (such as your email address and message body) are used solely to reply to your inquiry, investigate bug reports, or evaluate peer technical feedback.

⚖️ 7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Provisions)

Because BitDark.net executes tools client-side and requires no user registration, accounts, or persistent personal databases, we do not store, process, or sell your Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

Regarding third-party edge services (Cloudflare) and advertising cookies (Google AdSense), you maintain full rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) to inspect disclosures and opt out of behavioral targeting via the links provided in Section 4.
