How Automated OCR Pipelines Read Images

Optical Character Recognition systems extract text across sequential stages:

  • Binarization & Thresholding: Algorithms like Otsu or adaptive mean-thresholding convert gray levels to binary black-and-white to distinguish text from backgrounds.
  • Connected Component Analysis & Segmentation: The engine identifies continuous pixel clusters and segments them into discrete bounding boxes for character and word identification.
  • Feature Extraction & LSTM Neural Modeling: Segmented bounding matrices are evaluated through convolutional layers and bidirectional LSTMs to predict character sequences and synthesize text tokens.

Adversarial Attack Vectors Applied by This Tool

To thwart OCR algorithms while keeping the message decipherable to human perceptual systems, this tool executes multi-layer adversarial rendering:

  • High-Frequency Salt-and-Pepper Speckle: Random pixel noise shifts local contrast levels, preventing standard edge detectors from finding stable character contours.
  • Geometric Segmentation Breakers: Intersecting micro-lines bridge adjacent glyphs and split individual strokes, causing bounding box segmentation routines to fail.
  • Affine Glyph Shear & Baseline Jitter: Characters are perturbed with individual vertical shifts and rotational offsets, neutralizing static horizontal line slicing.
