Why Client-Side Email Redaction Matters

Organizations and developers often need to share logs, issue reports, or debugging traces without violating data protection standards like GDPR, HIPAA, or CCPA. Traditional online redaction services upload your unmasked sensitive files to remote servers, exposing PII to intermediate network logs and third-party storage vectors.

Redaction Algorithms Implemented

  • Partial Masking: Retains the first and last character of the mailbox handle and substitutes internal characters with masking tokens (e.g., j***n@domain.com), allowing human reviewers to preserve context while blocking automated web scrapers.
  • Domain-Preserving Anonymization: Erases the mailbox name completely while retaining domain routing paths, useful for domain-level metric aggregation and infrastructure debugging.
  • Deterministic Pseudonymization: Computes a truncated SHA-256 digest of normalized addresses, ensuring recurring identifiers remain linkable across datasets without exposing actual user addresses.
