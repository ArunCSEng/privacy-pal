⚠️ Forensic Caution & Authenticity Boundaries

Absence of C2PA metadata does not prove human authorship. Provenance containers are fragile: standard social media platforms (X, Instagram, WhatsApp), messaging pipelines, screenshots, and simple image re-encoders strip JUMBF headers immediately. Conversely, a present claim demonstrates that an asset was stamped by a conforming generator (e.g., Copilot, DALL-E, Firefly), but does not guarantee the file was not altered post-generation if the cryptographic signature hash has been broken. Always cross-reference client-side manifests with full X.509 authority validation. How C2PA Provenance Operates

The Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) defines an open technical standard allowing publishers, creators, and AI model engines (such as Adobe Firefly, OpenAI DALL-E 3, Midjourney, and Google Imagen) to embed cryptographically verifiable claims directly into asset binaries.

  • JPEG/AVIF/HEIC Encodings: Manifests are stored inside isolated application segments (such as APP11 with identifier c2pa or JUMBF boxes).
  • PNG Containers: Embedded as private chunks labeled c2pa or standard ancillary metadata chunks (eXIf, zTXt).
  • ISO-BMFF Video (MP4): Embedded within the root container under the uuid box type identifying the CAI signature GUID.
