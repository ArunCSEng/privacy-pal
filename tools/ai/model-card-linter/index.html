⚠️ Documentation Audit Boundaries & Operational Disclaimer

A compliant model card does not guarantee an aligned or secure model. This utility performs static heuristic analysis on documentation declarations, YAML frontmatter schemas, and safety disclosures. It cannot audit runtime model weights, test against zero-day adversarial jailbreaks, or verify whether safety claims match empirical runtime behavior. Why Model Card Security Audits Matter

Model cards, originally proposed by Margaret Mitchell et al. (Google Research), represent the foundational transparency standard for open and closed model releases. In modern AI defense, an unvetted or incomplete model card poses critical operational risks:

  • Unintended Use Boundaries: Clear delineation of where a model must never be deployed (e.g., automated biometric scoring, life-critical actuation, unmonitored medical advice).
  • Data Provenance & Copyright Risk: Explicit accounting of training sets, data filtering steps, and consent mechanisms to mitigate copyright infringement liability.
  • Adversarial & Safety Disclosures: Transparency regarding known vulnerability surfaces, red-teaming methodologies, and prompt-injection mitigations.
