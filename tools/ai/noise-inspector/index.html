⚠️ Forensic Caution & Algorithmic Boundaries

Residual noise inspection is a heuristic diagnostic and cannot provide absolute proof of AI generation. Diffusion models (such as Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, or DALL-E) frequently introduce characteristic checkerboard grid patterns or unnaturally uniform background sensor noise. However, aggressive lossy JPEG compression, camera sensor noise at high ISO, post-processing filters, and social media downscalers produce similar spatial artifacts. Never use high-pass residuals in isolation to establish definitive authenticity without cross-referencing cryptographic provenance (C2PA) and authentic capture metadata. How Diffusion Noise Inspection Operates

Latent Diffusion Models construct images iteratively by predicting and removing Gaussian noise conditioned on latent prompts. This leaves telltale statistical fingerprints in the spatial domain:

  • High-Frequency Residuals: Applying an unsharp mask or high-pass convolution kernel subtracts low-frequency structures, exposing synthetic noise distributions and tiling seams.
  • Laplacian Second-Order Derivation: Highlights sudden shifts in gradient curvature, identifying unnatural boundary sharpness often seen around synthetic contours.
  • Error Level Analysis (ELA): Approximates recompression tolerances across uniform areas to detect local variance mismatches between synthetic subject insertions and natural backgrounds.
