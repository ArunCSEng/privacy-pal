What Tokens Are

Language models don't read characters or words. They read tokens: chunks of text produced by a tokenizer, usually a byte-pair encoding (BPE) trained on a large amount of text. Common English words are often a single token, including the space before them. Rare words, code, numbers and many non-English scripts are split into more pieces. API pricing and context limits are both counted in tokens.

As a rough guide for English, OpenAI suggests about 4 characters, or three-quarters of a word, per token. Each model family uses its own tokenizer, so the same text gives different counts on GPT, Claude, Gemini and Llama models.

How This Calculator Estimates

This tool doesn't run a real tokenizer. It estimates with simple rules:

  • Words of up to 4 letters count as 1 token; longer words count as their length divided by about 3.7.
  • Each run of punctuation counts as at least 1 token.
  • Each space counts as a token (runs of spaces count 1 per 4).
  • Any word containing a non-ASCII character counts 1 token per character, so "café" counts as 4.

From that estimate it shows the share of the selected model's context window used and an input cost at the price per million tokens you enter.

What the Cost Figure Leaves Out

  • Output tokens. Responses are billed separately and usually at a higher rate than input.
  • Hidden tokens. Chat formatting, tool definitions, images, and reasoning tokens on reasoning models all add to the real count.
  • Discounts. Prompt caching and batch processing can cut input costs substantially on many APIs.
  • Room to reply. The context window must hold the prompt and the response, so leave space below the limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this token count?
It's a rough estimate that usually runs high. In our comparison with OpenAI's tokenizers, it gave 145 tokens for English text that really uses 62, and overcounted accented text about fourfold. Use it for ballpark planning and the provider's tokenizer for exact numbers.
How many tokens is 1,000 words?
For typical English with OpenAI's tokenizers, roughly 1,300 to 1,400 tokens, using the rule of thumb of about three-quarters of a word per token. Code, numbers, unusual words and other languages use more. Other model families' tokenizers give somewhat different counts.
Why do Hindi, Chinese or accented words cost more tokens?
Tokenizers are trained mostly on English, so other scripts are often split into smaller pieces. Newer tokenizers, such as GPT-4o's o200k_base, are more efficient: our Hindi sentence used 36 tokens there against 63 with GPT-4's tokenizer.
Are the model prices up to date?
Not necessarily. Prices and context limits change often and the model list covers earlier releases, so enter the current input rate from your provider's pricing page. The cost shown also excludes output tokens.
Is my prompt sent to any AI service?
No. The estimate is calculated in your browser, and the tool makes no network requests with your text, so it's safe to paste confidential prompts.
