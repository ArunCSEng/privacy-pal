Unix Crontab Syntax & Field Specification

Standard Unix crontab configuration files follow a five-field format separated by whitespace:

  • * * * * * command_to_execute
  • Minute (0-59): Exact minute an automation triggers.
  • Hour (0-23): 24-hour military notation hour.
  • Day of Month (1-31): Numeric calendar day of the month.
  • Month (1-12 or JAN-DEC): Calendar month.
  • Day of Week (0-6 or SUN-SAT): Day index where both 0 and 7 correspond to Sunday in POSIX/Vixie implementations.

Special Characters & Supported Operators

  • Asterisk (*): Matches every possible value within the designated field.
  • Comma (,): Specifies a discrete list of explicit values (e.g., 1,15,30).
  • Hyphen (-): Defines a continuous integer range (e.g., 1-5 for Monday through Friday).
  • Slash (/): Denotes step increments across a range or wildcard (e.g., */10 triggers every 10 units).
